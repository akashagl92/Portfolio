# The Role
We’re looking for a Product Enthusiast — someone who operates at the intersection of product thinking and hands-on building. You’ll work closely with one of our founders to turn ideas into shipped features, moving quickly from concept to production.

The product development lifecycle is evolving rapidly with the rise of AI-native tools. You don’t need to know everything on day one — we’ll train you to take scoped product tickets and turn them into production-ready code using modern AI-assisted development workflows.

This role is ideal for someone who wants to grow into a senior product or technical leadership position at an early-stage startup.
