# Texas Instruments - Senior Manager, Advertising Strategy & Intelligence, AI Technologies
**Date**: 2026-05-14

## Job Description / User Prompt
Senior Manager, Advertising Strategy & Intelligence, AI Technologies  
Reports to: Head of Brand Marketing and External Channels

The role is responsible for defining, operationalizing, and scaling AI across external marketing and advertising efforts to drive business growth. Serves as the organization's AI authority for brand marketing and external channels.

Primary responsibilities:
- Defining and executing AI transformation vision and roadmap for Brand Marketing & External Channels
- Developing AI-driven advertising strategies and workflows (ABM, intent-signal activation, account-based personalization)
- Building advanced full-funnel advertising intelligence (audience signals, campaign performance, BI)
- Leading modern approaches to advertising measurement and attribution strategy
- Collaborating with IT, CDP, Data Warehouse, and agency partners

Requirements:
- 8+ years in advertising, marketing technology, or digital marketing
- BA in Marketing, Business, Data Analytics, or CS
- Preferred: SQL, GA4, BI tools, CDP, DSP, ABM, LLM applications, agentic workflows, predictive modeling
