# Hershey - Senior Manager, Data Products
**Date**: 2026-03-12

## Job Description / User Prompt
Building, scaling, and operating enterprise-grade data products that serve as trusted, reusable assets across Hershey’s business domains. Ensuring data products are technically strong, well-architected, governed by design, and adoption-ready, enabling consistent analytics, reporting, and decision-making at scale.

Enterprise leadership role sitting at the intersection of data engineering, architecture, governance, and business domain expertise. Accountable for the end-to-end lifecycle of data products — from intake and design through delivery, certification, and ongoing operations.

## Key Themes to Emphasize
- [x] **Enterprise Data Systems**: Building durable, scalable "systems of record" for analytics and AI.
- [x] **Data Quality & Governance**: Implementing certification, lineage, and "governed by design" principles.
- [x] **Reuse & Scalability**: Shifting from one-off reports to reusable enterprise data products.
- [x] **Agentic Foundations**: Preparing data assets as the backbone for future AI use cases.
- [x] **Engineering Leadership**: Leading multi-disciplinary teams (engineers, analysts, product managers) to deliver complex portfolios.
