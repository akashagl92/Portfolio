# EY-Parthenon - AI Labs Product Management - Director
**Date**: 2026-02-05

## Job Description / User Prompt
Within the EY-Parthenon service line, the AI Labs Product Manager will collaborate with Business Leaders, AI/ML Engineers, Data Scientists, Project Managers, and other team members to design, build, and scale innovative AI solutions that power strategic growth initiatives.

### Key Responsibilities
- Lead development of data-driven, AI-powered agent applications.
- Build multi-source insight workflows.
- Translate business needs into functional product specs, agent behavior, and user experiences.
- Own product roadmap and use case prioritization.
