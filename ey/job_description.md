# Job Description: AI Labs Product Management - Director @ EY-Parthenon

## About the job
**Location**: Atlanta, Boston, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, Detroit, Houston, Los Angeles, McLean, New York, Hoboken, Philadelphia, San Francisco, Seattle

EY-Parthenon – AI Labs Product Management - Director

## The opportunity
EY-Parthenon’s unique combination of transformative strategy, transactions and corporate finance delivers real-world value – solutions that work in practice, not just on paper. Within the EY-Parthenon service line, the AI Labs Product Manager will collaborate with Business Leaders, AI/ML Engineers, Data Scientists, Project Managers, and other team members to design, build, and scale innovative AI solutions that power strategic growth initiatives.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead development of data-driven, AI-powered agent applications.
- Build multi-source insight workflows.
- Translate business needs into functional product specs, agent behavior, and user experiences.
- Own product roadmap and use case prioritization based on technical feasibility, customer impact, and data availability.
- Define POC and MVP requirements and success metrics for each agent product or data application.
- Communicate progress, risks, and decisions with clarity across levels.

## Qualifications
- Bachelor’s degree in Business, Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, Engineering, Computer Science, Analytics, or related field + 5 years experience; or Graduate degree + 3 years experience.
- 5-8+ years of product management experience.
- Experience in data-intensive or AI/ML-driven platforms.
- Understanding of data product lifecycle (ingestion, cleaning, merging, modeling, serving).
- Familiarity with agentic AI workflows, RAG, or data fusion.
- Passion for innovation and deep understanding of market trends in AI.
